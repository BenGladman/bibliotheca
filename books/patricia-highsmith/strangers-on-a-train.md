Guy, a regular kind of guy, meets a psychopath, Bruno, and is drawn into being accomplice to the murder of his own soon-to-be-ex wife and committing murder himself. The main part of the story is how Guy psychologically falls apart after this, and Bruno becomes obsessed with Guy.
