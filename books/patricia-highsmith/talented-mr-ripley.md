Ripley starts off a small time crook. He goes to see a rich old acquaintance in Italy, and becomes obsessed with him and his wealth and lifestyle. Even with all Ripley’s jealousy, selfishness and lack of morality you get to like him and root for him. He’s sharp and clever, and lucky maybe a bit too often. Even the worst things he does he can justify to himself as the only course of action which will let him get where he wants to be. It’s fun to be in the baddie’s head and on his side. And it helps that his victims don’t have a lot to recommend them.
