A few years after [Talented](the-talented-mr-ripley), Ripley has set up an art scam with some people in London. He’s living in a small castle in France with his wife, she is away on holiday for a lot of the action though. We see Ripley sweat a bit more in this episode, and he’s in more peril than before. He’s also quite lucky again, he’s acting on instinct a lot of the time and the other characters do exactly what they need to do for everything to work out well for him. But it’s great to see the plot strands come together.
