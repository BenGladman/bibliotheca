title: Kindred
author: Butler, Octavia E.
published: 1979
tags: [Fantasy, Slavery]
read: 2024
---

Dana, a black woman from the present day experiences slavery of 1800s through unexplained time travel which starts happening to her randomly and without warning when she moves house. There is a link between Dana and the slave owner family, especially Rufus the son, whom she is compelled to protect from harm. Through several episodes we see Rufus grow up and his white supremacy views solidify. Dana’s boyfriend is white and he also travels back in time.
