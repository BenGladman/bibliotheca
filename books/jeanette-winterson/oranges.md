It starts off with a comedic tone which can grate, like a Victoria Wood script every sentence seems to end with a pause for laughter. But at the chapter Deuteronomy it gets heavier as the narrator chooses to follow her own desires instead of the rules of the church in which she has been brought up. There are still comedy moments after this but it’s less full on, and better for that.

The story is fictionalised autobiography and I often found myself researching which bits are true instead of simply appreciating the story.
