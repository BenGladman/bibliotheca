Saleem Sinai tells the story of his life, which is somehow linked to the history of India and Pakistan. It is full of politics and allusions to Hindu mythology. No doubt many references went over my head, others led me to find out more. But importantly the story is fun to read and bounds along.

Saleem is born in Bombay at the same moment that India is born as an independent nation. He’s got a large nose and he’s telepathic with other children born at the same time. He has a wealthy family and a nemesis Shiva, who was born at the same time in the same place. Shiva has a poor family, huge knees and he’s great at fighting.
