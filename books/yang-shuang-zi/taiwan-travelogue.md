A lot of descriptions of food here, but the underlying theme is imperialism and power imbalance. The narrator has the advantage of being from Japan, the colonizing nation. She is blind to how that relationship looks from the other side, and why the friendship she desires is not possible.

The form of the book is interesting: we get footnotes notionally by the narrator, mixed with footnotes by the author, mixed with footnotes from the English translator.
