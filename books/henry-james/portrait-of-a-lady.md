There’s a good number of “idle rich” here, with Isabel and (especially) Henrietta asking them how they justify their lives. I am reading this just as I finish my employment contract and I try not to feel guilty. Henrietta is fun but so rude to people I wonder how she maintains her relationships. Everyone is just so superficially polite. Often people hold long conversations where they hold back what they mean, but not at all subtly. James is fond of a long sentence, did readers have better comprehension skills in the olden days? I get lost after the third clause and have to start again.
