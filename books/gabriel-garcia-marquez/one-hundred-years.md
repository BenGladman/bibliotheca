---
title: One Hundred Years of Solitude
author: García Márquez, Gabriel
year: 1967
tags: [Magical Realism, Colombia]
---

A family saga over several generations, and a story of a town. Some events in the book are based on the true history of Colombia.

Many people share names with each other to make it extra fun and confusing. There is a family tree at the start of the book which helps, but it also includes years of death which can be spoilers. The names people are given goes some way to shaping their character. There is quite a bit of incest going on, and a fear of child deformity because of it.
