---
title: Love in the Time of Cholera
author: García Márquez, Gabriel
published: 1985
location: Colombia
read: 2025
---

Florentino and Fermina are in love. Florentino is pretty obsessive. Her dad doesn’t approve. Fermina marries someone else. Florentino spends his life in love with Fermina, but also has many affairs and is generally quite misogynistic. When they are old, Fermina’s husband dies, and she and Florentino get back in touch.
