---
title: Rebecca
author: Du Maurier, Daphne
year: 1938
tags: [Gothic, Crime]
---

The narrator marries a very rich man, Maxim, whom she meets while she’s working as a companion for a rich woman. The rich woman is a gossip and a figure of fun but we don’t get to see much of her. The narrator is exasperated by her, and eager to move on from her current position which explains why she’s so easily swept away by Maxim.

The couple move to the Maxim’s massive family home in Cornwall. The housekeeper Mrs Danvers is infatuated with Maxim’s first wife, Rebecca, who died quite recently. The narrator is extremely meek, she is too timid to take on her new role, and assumes Maxim is still in love with Rebecca. You do wish she would assert herself more, and have a serious chat with Maxim... but I guess she is young.
