---
title: Jamaica Inn
author: Du Maurier, Daphne
year: 1936
tags: [Gothic]
---

Mary goes to Bodmin Moor to live with her aunt and uncle after her mum dies. There is lots of awful weather. Her uncle Joss is a terrible man but also he is haunted by the terrors he has perpetrated, especially when he is drunk which happens regularly. Her aunt is barely a shadow with no agency.

Mary is pretty feisty which enamours her to Joss. There’s no sexual menace here though.

Her uncle’s brother Jem lives nearby. He is a sexy, younger version of her uncle, rough and cheeky, not terrible. There is a reverend with albinism whose description is problematic. He pops up unexpectedly a couple of times, just at the right moment to help out.
