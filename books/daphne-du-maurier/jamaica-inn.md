title: Jamaica Inn
author: Du Maurier, Daphne
year: 1936
tags: [Gothic]

Mary goes to Bodmin Moor to live with her aunt and uncle after her mum dies. There is lots of awful weather. Her uncle Joss is a huge and terrible man but also he is haunted by the terrors he has perpetrated, especially when he is drunk which happens regularly. Her aunt is barely a shadow with no agency.

Mary is pretty feisty which enamours her to Joss. There’s no sexual menace here though. That felt unlikely to me but I think the author didn’t want to explore that. To some extent Joss protects Mary from the rest of his gang. Even though Mary hates Joss, for what he does for money and how he treats her aunt, she is also fascinated by him.

Her uncle’s brother Jem lives nearby. He is a sexy, younger version of her uncle, rough and cheeky. He doesn’t seem terrible but we and Mary are kept wondering if he is hiding his involvement. There is also a reverend with albinism. The description of his albinism is problematic. He pops up unexpectedly a couple of times, just at the right moment to help out.
